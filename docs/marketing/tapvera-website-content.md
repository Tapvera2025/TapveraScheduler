## Shared platform capabilities

### Employee management

Keep employee contact details, positions, departments and active status in a shared directory. Connect employees to sites and user accounts so scheduling and self-service use the same staff records. This helps managers maintain a consistent view of their workforce as people join, move between locations or leave.

### Operations dashboard

Bring daily staffing counts, attendance records and pending requests into a management overview. Compare rostered hours with recorded hours for today, the week or the month, and review attendance indicators such as late arrivals and early finishes. Managers can use this view to identify records or requests that need attention.

### Notifications and reminders

Keep staff informed with supported email notifications, including shift assignments, upcoming-shift reminders and request decisions. Automatic no-show detection can flag eligible shifts without a recorded start. Email delivery and scheduled jobs depend on the deployment configuration.

### Organisation and access management

Provide separate administrator, manager and employee experiences. Organisation-level module settings control which capabilities are available. Platform administrators can onboard organisations, manage organisation administrators and enable or disable modules, helping the platform support multiple business workspaces.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Tapvera Scheduler

Tapvera Scheduler is a workforce operations platform for managing employees, work sites, schedules, attendance and everyday staff requests. It combines management tools with an employee self-service portal.

### Can we manage more than one client or site

Yes. You can maintain multiple client and site records, assign employees to sites and organise shifts by location within your organisation's workspace.

### Can employees use it on a phone

Yes. The responsive web interface supports mobile screen sizes. Location-based clock-ins require browser location permission, and voice features depend on compatible browser capabilities and microphone permission.

### How do location checks work

The system compares the location supplied at clock-in or clock-out with the site's configured coordinates and radius. A site must have a geofence configured for this boundary check to apply.

### Can we export attendance records

Yes. Managers can filter time records and export them as a CSV file for review and use in downstream processes. Tapvera Scheduler does not calculate wages or run payroll.

### Does the assistant change records automatically

Supported changes are presented for review and require confirmation before they are applied. Available actions depend on the user's role and the organisation's enabled modules. Natural-language and voice features require the relevant service configuration.

### Can we choose which capabilities our organisation uses

Modules can be enabled for each organisation by the platform administrator. Some capabilities depend on others, such as scheduling relying on site and client records. Your enabled modules determine the workflows available in your workspace.
